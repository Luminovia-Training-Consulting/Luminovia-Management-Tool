## Complete remaining product and release scope

The implementation plan's module ledger is authoritative. In particular, keep these requirements visible:

- Richer permission-aware global search and discovery, saved views, directory organisation and complete profile/qualification lifecycle.
- Calendar interaction, invitations/reminders and optional ICS; broader chat groups, moderation, search, subscriptions and resource/file retention/sharing lifecycle.
- Wiki/forum editorial and moderation completeness, course/participant resources, commercial document renewal and broader finance reports/bulk workflows.
- Durable approved email delivery/retries, preferences and quiet hours, announcements and optional platform-appropriate web push.
- Administrative retention, recoverable bulk operations, settings, diagnostics, complete backups and recurring off-site retention.
- Broader browser/device/PWA installation, manual accessibility, realistic capacity, hosted SMTP and operational restore/reboot acceptance, security/privacy/legal review and separately authorised production deployment.

These items include partly implemented areas: check the detailed ledger before opening an issue. Preserve immutable accepted deals, private DMs/calendars/contracts, server-side authorisation, DE/EN interfaces and authored content language throughout. [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to propose a bounded, testable increment.
