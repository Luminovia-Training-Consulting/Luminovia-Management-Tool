# Client accounts and private commission tracking

This continuation adds approved client accounts and an owner role to the existing invitation-only portal. It retains the original database authority, private-message/calendar boundaries and immutable accepted lecturer fees. It does not implement tax calculation, cash collection, banking or accounting revenue recognition.

## Roles and organisation membership

`client` accounts are linked to organisations by `client_organisation_memberships`. Only `users.roles.manage` with current AAL2 can add/remove those links. Reads and requests use current database membership, current client role, active profile and a nonarchived organisation. Forged user metadata and remembered browser IDs confer no authority. Removing a membership or role, or suspending the profile, revokes subsequent requests and spending reads with existing tokens.

The `owner` role receives existing administrator capabilities. `users.roles.manage` and `finance.gross.read` are granted only to admin/owner. Management retains authorised net-invoice operations but gains no gross-price reporting. Administrative/owner functional-role checks permit their own teaching/participant workflows; actual role rows do not change and privileged capabilities still require MFA. Qualification, contract, schedule and real course-enrolment checks continue to apply. This does not grant access to another person's private calendar or membership-free DM. The client organisation helper specifically requires an actual client role and organisation membership, rather than the functional administrator fallback.

## Requests and commissioned offers

Clients submit a bounded course/job request for their own organisation, including authored title/description, intended IANA zone, one to sixty explicit future sessions, topics and the full customer quote in original EUR or AUD. Requests are durable and actor/client-UUID idempotent. The closed payload rejects status, internal net fields, commission and lecturer agreement settings. It validates exact cents, fixed-total consistency, finite instants and nonoverlapping sessions. A submitted request can be cancelled; already published work requires the broader audited job lifecycle rather than silent cancellation.

Admin/owner publishes a client request through `publish_client_request`, or creates a complete offer transactionally through **`create_commission_job`**, the canonical new price-entry API. An existing organisation may be selected, or the authorised admin/owner may create an inline customer in the same transaction. Draft/published creation and durable retry identity are preserved. There is never an intermediate publicly visible job containing a gross price.

The default platform share is **50%**, leaving 50% for the lecturer. Admin/owner may configure each unaccepted offer with a percentage from 0 to 100, to two decimal places. The platform share is rounded to cents; lecturer net is the residual, so total gross always equals platform plus lecturer net, including odd-cent totals. Unit and total quote amounts are separately recorded as in the existing negotiated-fee model; the application does not invent a tax-inclusive total or a currency conversion. Reconfiguration locks the same job row as claiming and increments the terms version. A stale claim must refresh. Once any claim exists, commission reconfiguration is refused.

## Private gross and immutable accepted net

Structured customer gross/platform values live only in **unexposed private-schema tables**: `client_course_requests`, `job_commission_offers` and `claim_revenue_ledger`. These tables enable RLS, have no anonymous/authenticated table grants and are not published to Realtime. Public wrappers use invoker security; restricted private implementations use empty search paths and explicit current actor/organisation checks.

Lecturer-readable `jobs.amount`, `jobs.total_amount`, claims, earnings statements and invoice calculations remain **lecturer net**. No gross or platform column is added to their REST relations. Audit/notification entries contain action/resource metadata rather than gross pricing. Gross reports require `finance.gross.read` with MFA. Clients see their own organisation's customer prices, requests, spending and minimal assigned lecturer names, never another organisation's records or a lecturer's personal invoice/contract.

The existing atomic claim routine inserts a private revenue ledger row in the same transaction as allocation. Its net amounts must match the public accepted claim. Qualification/contract/conflict failures roll back the ledger too. A successful retry cannot duplicate revenue, allocations or notifications. Accepted revenue rows resist update and retain exact gross, platform percentage/share, net, currency, organisation, lecturer and claim references. The existing net-claim/invoice snapshots retain their protections.

## Legacy data and reports

The compatible `create_job` API still means a **net lecturer offer**, as it did before this continuation. It remains useful for existing operations; it does not imply a commission model. Previously accepted jobs are never retroactively split or repriced. `commission_report` identifies such rows as `pricing_model='legacy_net'`, with gross/platform values **null/unknown** and the known accepted/source net. New commissioned rows have `pricing_model='commission'`. Unknown gross is not zero revenue and must not be summed as measured gross.

Spending and assigned-teacher summaries group EUR/AUD separately and scope every organisation to the caller. Published counts follow actual published/filled job status; converted drafts remain uncounted until publication. Their values are quoted/committed amounts, not confirmed customer payments or recognised accounting revenue. The current request list has an explicit 500-row limit; larger operational pagination remains future scope. Legacy net-only jobs have no invented customer quote in client spending. Changes to an unaccepted commission update the visible client quote; accepted rows use their immutable ledger values.

Invoice labels are mutable organisation metadata. `set_invoice_labels` accepts at most ten distinct trimmed labels of one to fifty characters, rejects control characters and permits only the active invoice owner or current MFA finance operations. It logs the resource change without changing accepted amounts, dates, PDF version, claim references, status or payments. Direct client table writes remain denied.
