The first full scan of `main` at `d3ace919` reported **28 open findings: 12 High and 16 Medium**. The preceding PR scan found no new findings in its diff; that did not establish that the entire repository was free of warnings. All scanner jobs completed successfully. This page records a bounded source review, not a complete security certification. Findings were not dismissed in bulk, hidden or removed through path exclusions.
