# Luminovia Portal — product specification

This document records current product requirements and distinguishes application behaviour from acceptance evidence. See the [roadmap](FEATURES.md) and [readiness ledger](PRODUCTION-READINESS.md) for active scope and evidence.

The 16 November [smart operations extension](PRODUCT.md#section-smart-operations-spec) covers the Control Center, personalized dashboard/Teaching Day, transactional Hostinger email and optional mobile Push, waitlists and urgent teaching cover, timesheets, client/participant self-service, favourites, saved filters, calendar feeds, preparation, feedback and offline schedule.

<a id="section-product-spec-product-and-operating-model"></a>

## Product and operating model

A private, bilingual German/English, responsive PWA for freelance lecturers, administrators/management and scoped company accounts. Verified participant-only accounts and enrolments are scoped to each enabled company’s Premium LMS. Historical records remain protected. Management publishes teaching jobs. An authenticated, eligible lecturer may claim an entire job immediately; the first committed database transaction wins. The claim requires no routine approval queue. Client/employer organisations have explicit
individual company accounts and scoped memberships; see [Accounts and commission extension](PRODUCT.md#section-accounts-revenue-spec).

The original brief names `portal.luminovia.org` as the production target. Later accepted development uses the self-hosted Supabase tooling in this repository; the initial managed-backend default is superseded for that environment. Production architecture, capacity, processor/location review and release approval remain separate gates. No production deployment is authorised by the development task. The public marketing site is a read-only brand source.

<a id="section-product-spec-short-notice-assignment-withdrawals--17-november-2026"></a>

## Short-notice assignment withdrawals — 17 November 2026

Lecturers may directly withdraw from a confirmed future teaching block with at
least seven days (168 elapsed hours) before its first session. With less notice,
they must supply a reason in a withdrawal application. Administration or Management
reviews it and approves or rejects with a decision note; the author cannot review
their own application. Pending/rejected applications leave the whole assignment
binding. Approval releases the entire block while preserving accepted commercial
history. Decisions appear in the personal inbox. Started or invoiced work needs
separate review. [Workflow contract](GUIDE.md#section-assignment-withdrawals).

<a id="section-product-spec-roles-and-privacy-boundaries"></a>

## Roles and privacy boundaries

Current roles are `owner`, `admin`, `management`, `senior_lecturer`, `lecturer`, `client`, `participant` and `guest`. Management may assign permitted subordinate roles. Administration may also assign Management and may change an administrator peer's rank where the action allows it. Self/owner, activation, deletion and last-administrator protections are action-specific. One account may hold several roles. Company and teaching access still require the applicable organisation or teaching assignment. Company-facing and lecturer-facing financial views use their own permissions and resource scopes. Guest grants no business capabilities. An installation can require native TOTP for administrators and owners. Senior lecturer status alone confers no access to other lecturers' contracts or rates. Private direct messages and personal calendar entries retain their membership and ownership boundaries. Deactivation revokes access even while an existing token is valid.

The current individual lecturer setting defaults to 50 percent of a new commissioned offer or, when explicitly selected, EUR 200 per local teaching day. Authorised administration can save a different private percentage or daily rate through a versioned API. The earlier role-priced policy and accepted offer snapshots remain historical records; changing a current setting does not rewrite an accepted agreement. Actual business values must not be committed to this public repository or browser
bundles. Do not infer missing historical client fees. See [pricing](../API_REFERENCE.md#recent-settings-and-action-contracts).

New accounts receive a sequential, permission-aware DE/EN [welcome guide](GUIDE.md#section-welcome-guide), with private server-persisted progress, pause/resume, skip and restart. PRs retain required quality/security checks and conservatively selected docs/standard/full acceptance profiles. A genuinely runnable [local preview package](../CONTRIBUTING.md) records its actual profile; GitHub deployment metadata requires a separate explicit manual action. A downloadable package is not
a hosted preview website.

### LMS implementation directive

The LMS target extends the existing course runs, programmes, enrolments, assessment records, scanned resources, attendance, certificates and analytics through the complete author → publish → enrol → learn → assess → grade → feedback → verify → complete → report lifecycle. Configurable grading, immutable historical evidence, scoped permissions, accessibility and integration standards are requirements, not claims that the current source already implements them. The first partially implemented P0 vertical is module/lesson authoring with secure editor JSON, versioned publication, pinned enrolment versions and explicit migration. The [LMS delivery backlog](LMS-BACKLOG.md) records package ownership, dependencies, acceptance criteria and actual verification; do not treat this authoring slice as full LMS acceptance.

<a id="section-product-spec-critical-user-journeys"></a>

## Critical user journeys

1. Management invites a fictional staging lecturer; lecturer signs in, edits profile/expertise and uploads permitted evidence; forged document/profile requests are denied.
2. Management creates customer, course template/run, five sessions and an offered job with tags/rate/attachments; eligible lecturer combines filters and sees accurate local time and compensation.
3. Fifty simultaneous single-slot claims produce one committed winner, one complete allocation, one event/notification set; winner retries are idempotent. Expired contracts, missing eligibility and conflicting appointments reject clearly.
4. A lecturer creates a private event; peers and ordinary admins cannot read it. Published global events appear only to their intended audience. Berlin and Brisbane DST cases are correct.
5. DMs, lecturer course discussion and participant discussion each enforce distinct membership; attachments and realtime revoke with membership.
6. Authorised materials download/preview works; guessed file paths cannot reveal another lecturer's CV, contract or invoice.
7. Wiki/forum authoring, search, revision and moderation obey permissions and preserve code safely.
8. Accepted price/currency/terms do not change with job template edits. Finance records and exports follow resource permissions.
9. German/English, light/dark/system, desktop/mobile and supported PWA installation work. Offline writes reject without false success and sensitive caches remain empty.
10. Unit/component/browser, migration, database/RLS, concurrency, accessibility and adversarial tests run with recorded evidence. Untested features stay labelled untested.

<a id="section-smart-operations-spec-delivery-and-communication"></a>

## Delivery and communication

Hostinger Mail, with `info@luminovia.org` as the intended sender, is the selected application-email provider. Credentials belong only in private operating configuration. No supplied mailbox credential is copied to source, examples, logs or this guide. The actual server/timer/provider proof and activation state are owned by [Mail operations](../deploy/hostinger/README.md) and QA results, not inferred from an email-settings form or a successful unit test.

Notifications originate from committed business events. The own inbox and operational receipts remain available independently of optional external channels. Email preferences, quiet hours and IANA zone use current identity and CAS. Private email/Push outboxes deduplicate occurrences, lease bounded work and recheck current recipient/resource/channel eligibility before dispatch. Known temporary failures have bounded retry; uncertain provider acceptance requires reconciliation rather than blind
resend. SMTP acceptance alone is not proof of inbox delivery. Web Push is implemented with explicit browser/device consent, removable owner-bound subscriptions, minimal generic content and server-side provider/encryption checks. A permission-refusing headless browser or mock provider does not establish real desktop/mobile notification delivery.

Staff contact sharing is an explicit own email/phone opt-in, private by default, with current staff-only directory projections and safe actions. It creates no administrative consent override or client/participant directory grant. The separately selected course customer contact is an explicit same-organisation course setting, not a fallback to private Auth/profile data or every customer-directory contact.

# Accounts, course profiles and commission model

Historical approved extension: 16 November 2026 (Australia/Brisbane). It introduced client accounts and private role-based commission rules. The later current source uses individual lecturer settings, with a 50-percent default or an explicitly selected EUR-200 daily mode, and supports participant-only accounts through enabled companies. <a id="section-accounts-revenue-spec-identity-and-administrative-authority"></a>

## Identity and administrative authority

Each person has one Supabase Auth identity. Authorised administrators can assign several roles to the same account. The application offers the functions of each assigned role. PostgreSQL checks the current role, active account and the relevant organisation, assignment or course membership on protected requests. A company role needs an organisation association. A participant role needs a course enrolment for access to that course.

Owner and administrator roles cover business administration. Management coordinates assignments and courses under its operational permissions. The lecturer or senior lecturer role is required to accept a teaching offer personally or submit a lecturer’s own invoice. The existing authorised finance workflow for submitting an invoice on behalf of a lecturer continues to require an active lecturer as the invoice owner. An installation operator can stage and require native authenticator pairing for administrators and owners.

Private DM membership and private calendar ownership are retained. Full administrative functionality does not fabricate attendance, rewrite accepted work, grant a private DM membership or bypass financial transaction safeguards. Last/self-administrator protections serialize activation and role changes under the same database lock.

New fictional local fixtures add `carina@example.invalid` (owner demonstration) and `client@example.invalid` alongside the eleven lecturers, admin and optional learner. They have distinct Auth IDs. The shared generated fixture password is local QA setup only; invitation recipients set their own passwords. Existing passwords, roles, profile edits, suspensions and revoked memberships are never reset by seeding/startup. Existing development data can retain its original fixture set until the
explicit insert-only seed is run; startup never reseeds a populated instance automatically.

Gross and platform data live in an unexposed private schema. Commissioned offers use zero sentinels in raw `jobs.amount/total_amount`; applications use `job_offers()` for the current lecturer's individual quote. Lecturers receive only their own applicable fee; Management receives null for protected compensation. Accepted claims and lecturer invoices retain immutable lecturer-compensation semantics. Compensation rounds to cents first and the platform receives the exact remainder, without currency conversion. EUR, AUD, USD and CHF are supported. Historical offer-policy snapshots remain for provenance; current individual settings apply to future quotes. Explicit unclaimed-offer repricing advances its terms version. Recipient-specific quote versions and database locks prevent accepting an outdated price. Accepted gross/net/margin snapshots never change with later templates, roles or settings edits.

Existing net-only agreements remain unchanged. Their historical customer gross is **unknown**, represented as null/legacy rather than an invented double of net pay. Revenue reports distinguish offered/accepted values and preserve original currencies. A recorded lecturer payment does not prove customer funds were received, and does not initiate a bank transfer.

<a id="section-accounts-revenue-spec-course-and-profile-workflows"></a>

## Course and profile workflows

- Clients create scoped course requests with precise sessions and the full fee. Authorised administration publishes the lecturer offer. Eligible lecturers still claim immediately and atomically; no routine approval queue for lecturer claims is introduced.
- Staff profiles expose intentional biography, country/languages/teaching experience, authored labels, expertise and real upcoming/current/past assignments. Private contact details, CVs, contracts and rates are excluded. Client/learner profile projections are resource scoped. A full-course link appears only if the actor can open that course.
- Profile labels are self-authored and are not qualification verification or permissions. Directory filters use these labels and actual topics. Invoices have their own bounded labels and combined search/status/currency/label filters and exports.
- Managers/admins assign optional learners to courses. Reviews require confirmed completed enrolment and a real assigned lecturer, reject self reviews, preserve version history/audit and hide learner identity from ordinary readers. Moderation requires its capability and a reason. No fabricated attendance or manually invented reviews are seeded.
