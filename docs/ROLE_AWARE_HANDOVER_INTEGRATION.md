# Recipient-specific handover integration

Migration `20261116045011` integrates substitution and forecasting with the private role-pricing contract from `20261116020953`. It adds no operational policy or default percentage and changes neither the released `31429`/`32842` sources nor accepted historical fees/revenue. The original customer gross, organisation and currency also remain a required ledger invariant; a mismatch requires financial review.

A proposal captures a new recipient's own applicable net amount from the job's captured private policy, together with the complete immutable teaching block. The private `pricing_context` retains the role discriminator, captured policy identity and exact revenue facts. The public quote contains only that recipient's net and ordinary assignment terms. Source lecturers, proposers and ordinary management receive operational progress with monetary/terms fields and the quote hash redacted. The hash is also private because a price fingerprint could otherwise support guessing. Current administrative gross-finance permission and MFA remain the separate financial inspection boundary.

Acceptance takes the existing lecturer profile/advisory/job/claim/offer locks and rechecks the entire pricing context before inserting a new claim. Forward migration `20261116051736` also rechecks current management withdrawal permission after those locks: a queued request cannot retain authority revoked while it waited. Immutable source/proposer withdrawal rights remain valid for an active account. A recipient role change or captured-offer change returns `terms_changed`; a new immutable offer and new consent are required. Global policy changes continue to affect future offers, while a job's existing captured policy remains stable. Independent post-insert checks require the actual new claim and revenue ledger to match the consented deal exactly. A discrepancy rolls back the whole transfer. Old fees, revenue, invoice history and accepted evidence remain unchanged.

Pending historical offers without a recipient pricing context become `superseded` during upgrade. Their immutable history remains available; they cannot silently acquire newly calculated fees. The existing no-invoice-history, future-only, qualification, contract, availability, full-session and membership-revocation rules remain effective.

For forecasting, a role-dependent job's public zero is a privacy sentinel. Its open-position cost is returned as `null` with `pipeline_cost_unknown_positions`, including mixed monthly buckets. The UI displays an explicit unknown amount and count in German and English. Accepted claim costs still use actual immutable amounts. No future regular/senior mix, alternate rate, currency conversion or private percentage is invented or disclosed.

The focused local evidence is sixteen real PostgreSQL integration groups, the existing fifteen handover groups, fourteen finance groups, thirteen component tests, typecheck and lint. The integration cases cover both tier directions, exact odd-cent revenue conservation, private audience projections, stale and concurrent role changes, captured-policy drift, future-only global policy changes, historical pending closure and unknown prospective costs. The new REST suite uses actual Auth/MFA/HTTP through the canonical disposable harness; execution requires the combined PR18 and forward schema, and is tracked separately from this local SQL/component evidence. No normal development/hosted data, operational policy or real SMTP is used.

The withdrawal regression observes both actual PostgreSQL sessions: a role mutation holds the manager profile through a controlled pre-commit barrier, and the withdrawal is confirmed to wait on a lock. After revocation commits, the foreign manager receives `42501` and the offer, assignment, sessions, notifications and related audit remain identical. A negative-control run against released `45011` reproduces the formerly successful stale withdrawal; no released migration bytes are edited.

Forward migration `20261116052910` makes accepted-offer retries return the current target-owned resulting claim status. After later cancellation, an exact consent retry returns `cancelled` with the same claim ID, and cannot create another calendar reservation, notice or audit event. The original exact-hash and explicit-consent requirements remain effective even for historical retries. The focused native test and actual REST regression cover the full accept–cancel–retry sequence; a bilingual component test prevents the cancelled response from being announced as confirmation.

Forward migration `20261116054135` requires current lecturer authority on historical acceptance retries, after the shared actor-profile lock and the exact hash/consent checks. An active account that lost its lecturer role receives `not_eligible`; suspension remains `42501`. Native checks cover both sequential changes and two observed profile-lock races for role and activation revocation, with unchanged business records/calendars/notices. The same issued-token role/activation cases are included in the actual REST suite. Earlier migration bytes are retained exactly.
